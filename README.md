# SlyNX

*(pronounced "slinx")*

Native ports of the Sly Cooper trilogy for the Nintendo Switch and PC, written from scratch in Rust. Bring your own
disc: the packager converts your own disc image into a Switch program on your own computer.

**No game data is included or ever committed.** You need your own copies of the games.

Status: early development.
